What the seven checks are

  1. Opening Balance Equity has activity this period.
  2. Negative A/R by customer or A/P by vendor, within the export.
  3. Undeposited Funds not clearing: a credit balance, or receipts older than 14 days.
  4. Possible duplicates: same bank account, amount and name within 3 days.
  5. Uncleared bank items older than 60 days. Needs the Clr column.
  6. Transfers whose other side is not a bank, card or cash account.
  7. Uncategorised, Ask My Accountant, or suspense postings.

Heuristics on an export, not a replacement for the reconciliation screen. Account types are inferred from names.
